# The World in One Image — Friday 27 November 2026

A newspaper front page summing up the world news of Friday 27 November 2026:

- **Ukraine** — Zelenskyy warns Russia may strike the power grid again; EU split over a €45bn loan for Kyiv; US approves $622m in military aid.
- **USA** — Thanksgiving storm cuts power to 724,000 on the east coast; flights grounded, bridges shut.
- **Egypt** — Fuel tanker fire kills 31 on a Cairo highway.
- **Kuwait** — Drone attack kills two Italian technicians; Iran denies responsibility.
- **Philippines** — Mount Mayon erupts, ash 3,000 metres high.
- **Black Friday** — Shoppers chase deeper discounts, essentials as well as gifts.
- **Space** — US clears SpaceX to launch up to 1m orbital data-centre satellites.
- **Diplomacy** — Trump says Putin "would like a deal" to end the war in Ukraine.

## How it runs

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) copies the finished `entry.png` to
`/output/entry.png` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png` when set). Both runs
write byte-identical output.
